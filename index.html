How to Start Freelancing

Freelancing allows people to offer services such as writing, graphic design, programming and virtual assistance to clients.

Start by choosing one skill, practising it, building sample projects and creating a professional portfolio. You can then explore platforms such as Fiverr and Upwork.

How AI Tools Can Improve Your Productivity

Artificial intelligence tools can help with research, brainstorming, writing, design and learning.

Use AI to support your work, verify important information and develop your own skills rather than relying on automatically generated material without review.
